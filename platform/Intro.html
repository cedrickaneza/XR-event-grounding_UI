What's captured

A procedure, broken down four ways.

01 · Parts

Raw pieces

The kit's atomic parts — nuts, washers, pins, wheels — as the operator unpacks them.

02 · Components

Subassemblies

Pre-built groups: chassis pins, brackets, wheel assemblies — the units the operator actually installs.

03 · Assembly

The build

A canonical target state reached by installing components in sequence — captured per clip in first-person XR video.

04 · Maintenance

Corrections

Detect-and-remove sequences when something is installed wrong — the messy reality the AI must handle.
